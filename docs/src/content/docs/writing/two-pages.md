In **Preview** with the window **full screen**, Marginal lays the document
out as two facing pages, like an open book, and shows nothing else: the
toolbar, sidebar, tabs, and status bar step aside, and on Windows and
Linux so does the menu bar.

## Turning it on

1. Open a document and switch to **Preview Only** (`Mod+3`).
2. Make the window full screen:
   - **macOS**: **View ▸ Enter Full Screen** (<kbd>⌃⌘F</kbd>) or the
     green window button.
   - **Windows and Linux**: **View ▸ Full Screen** (<kbd>F11</kbd>).

The pages appear as soon as the window is full screen. The
[reading view](../reading-view/) works the same way: make it full screen
and it turns into pages too.

## Turning pages

| To | Press |
|---|---|
| Next spread | <kbd>→</kbd>, <kbd>Page Down</kbd>, or <kbd>Space</kbd> |
| Previous spread | <kbd>←</kbd>, <kbd>Page Up</kbd>, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> |
| First / last spread | <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> |

One turn of the mouse wheel moves one spread, a sideways swipe on a
trackpad turns the page, and a click near the left or right edge of the
screen turns back or forward. Links, checkboxes, and the copy button on
code blocks work as usual.

Headings stay with the text that follows them, and code blocks, tables,
images, and callouts are never split across two pages. Long code lines
wrap instead of scrolling sideways. Each page is at most 720 pixels wide,
so lines stay a comfortable length on a large screen.

## Getting out

- **Leave full screen** (<kbd>⌃⌘F</kbd> on macOS; <kbd>Esc</kbd> or
  <kbd>F11</kbd> on Windows and Linux) to return to a normal window, still
  in Preview.
- **Switch view** with `Mod+1` (Editor) or `Mod+2` (Split) to bring the
  editor and the rest of the window back while staying full screen.

`Mod` is <kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS and <kbd>Alt</kbd> on Windows and Linux.

## Turning it off

**Settings ▸ Preview ▸ Layout ▸ Two pages in full screen** is on by
default. Turn it off to keep the usual scrolling preview in full screen.

## Platform notes

On macOS the menu bar stays hidden in full screen until you move the
pointer to the top of the screen, as in any app. The setting is not
offered on iPhone and iPad, which have no full-screen window; on the
iPhone Duo, holding the phone half open like a book gives the same two
facing pages.
